## The short version

Postie makes collages on your phone. Your photos never leave your device unless you choose to share or save them.

## Photos

- You choose photos with the iOS photo picker. Postie only receives the photos you pick and never browses your library.
- Postie keeps copies of the photos you pick inside the app, so your collages can be edited later. Deleting a collage in Postie removes those copies.
- When you tap Save, Postie asks for permission to add images to your photo library. It cannot read your library with this permission.

## Data we collect

None. Postie has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics and no tracking.

## Network

When it starts, Postie downloads a small settings file from [your domain]. The request contains no personal information, no identifiers and no cookies.

## Advertising

Postie does not show ads today. If ads are turned on in a future version, they will be non-personalized ads from Google AdMob, shown only after you save or share a collage, and this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated first. Where the law requires it, for example in the EEA and UK, you will be asked for consent before any ad is requested.

## Children

Postie is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.
